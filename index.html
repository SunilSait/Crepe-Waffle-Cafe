Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Flavor

Born from a love of Parisian creperies and the charm of Brussels waffle stands, Crepe & Waffle Café brings the soul of European street food to your neighborhood.

Every crepe is made on a traditional billig griddle, every waffle pressed in cast-iron Belgian irons, and every topping chosen for its freshness and flavor.
