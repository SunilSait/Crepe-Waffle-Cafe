The Art of the Parisian Crepe

Delicate, silken, and paper-thin with signature lacy crisp edges that melt seamlessly upon the palate.

  • 24-Hour Batter Cold Ferment Allows starches to hydrate fully, banishing gluten chewiness for featherlight silkiness.
  • The Hand-Spun Rozell Twirl Each portion is spread across a 200°C cast-iron billig in three smooth, unbroken sweeps.
  • Stoneground Sarrasin Buckwheat Our savory galettes are 100% gluten-free, crafted with heritage buckwheat from Brittany.
Belgian Heritage
Belgian Masterpiece • Liege Tradition

The Craft of the Liege Waffle

Crisp, caramelized amber exterior harboring a buttery, pillowy brioche cloud within.

  • Cultured Brioche Yeast Dough Never thin, watery pancake batter. We slow-proof a rich dough kneaded with European butter.
  • Imported Tienen Pearl Sugar Dense sugar pearls folded in by hand right before baking so they resist early dissolution.
  • Deep-Pocket Heavy Cast Iron Heavy iron plates melt the pearl sugar into an irresistible caramelized glaze.
